# 🎨 NEXORA AI — Brand Color Palette

### Veda Technology Internship — Task 3 | Graphic Design

A professional brand color palette created for **NEXORA AI**, a fictional AI technology brand.

The visual identity is designed to communicate **intelligence, innovation, trust, futuristic technology, accessibility, and professionalism** through a carefully selected color system.

---

## 🚀 Project Overview

This project focuses on creating a consistent and professional color system for a fictional AI technology company.

The palette combines deep blue foundations with vibrant cyan, purple, and lime accents to create a futuristic technology-focused visual identity while maintaining readability and visual hierarchy.

## 📊 Color Hierarchy

The visual system follows a **60 / 30 / 10** color hierarchy.

| Percentage | Color Role | Purpose |
|------------|------------|---------|
| 60% | Deep Space Blue / Neutral Foundation | Backgrounds and core visual structure |
| 30% | Quantum Teal / Supporting Colors | Highlights, interfaces and supporting elements |
| 10% | Neural Lime / Accent | Important interactions and attention areas |

This hierarchy helps maintain balance while allowing vibrant accent colors to stand out without overwhelming the design.

---

## 🧠 Color Theory

The NEXORA AI palette balances **professional depth with futuristic energy**.

Deep Space Blue establishes a foundation of trust, intelligence, and stability. Quantum Teal introduces a modern technological feeling, while Aurora Purple adds creativity and futuristic depth.

Neural Lime is used sparingly as a high-visibility accent to communicate progress, innovation, and important interactions.

The combination creates a strong visual hierarchy suitable for an AI and technology-focused brand.
